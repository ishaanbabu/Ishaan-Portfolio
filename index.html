My Time in iTec

iTec's been treating me really well, and I've learned so many new things, like 3D designing, website coding, photoshopping, etc. I didn't even know graphic designing was a thing. It's not just about learning, it's also about having fun. For example, we did things like Lego Robotics, doing lessons on Minecraft Education, and designing rocket ships in Minecraft. We also get to be with our friends. So far, I'm loving it, and can't wait for what we will be learning next.
